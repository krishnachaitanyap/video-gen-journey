# Papers

Local copies and annotated PDFs of key papers. Reading list lives in `CLAUDE.md` and the webapp Resources section — this folder is for the marked-up reading you keep coming back to.

Suggested per-paper layout:

- `YYYY-author-short-title/`
  - `paper.pdf` — original
  - `paper-annotated.pdf` — your markup (optional)
  - `notes.md` — what you took away (link to a fuller summary in `notes/papers/` if applicable)

**Phase 2 essentials** (master these): DDPM (Ho 2020), DDIM (Song 2021), CFG (Ho &amp; Salimans 2022), EDM (Karras 2022), Flow Matching (Lipman 2023), Rectified Flow (Liu 2023).

**Phase 3**: DiT (Peebles &amp; Xie 2022), Latent Diffusion (Rombach 2022), SD3 (Esser 2024).

**Phase 4**: Sora tech report, Movie Gen (90-pager, most educational), HunyuanVideo, CogVideoX, Wan 2.2.

> Don't commit PDFs you don't have the right to redistribute. For papers on arXiv that's fine; for paywalled or company-licensed material, keep local only and rely on the link in your `notes.md`.
